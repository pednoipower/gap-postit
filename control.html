The room so far

Counts leave out the practice round. While the board is closed nobody can add anything — useful between rounds so people watch the screen instead of typing.

Slides

The projector and every phone follow whatever you pick here. Each gap has three slides — why is this happening?, a minute of turn and talk, then how would we fix it? The two writing rounds collect blind (the projector shows only a count) until you press Reveal, which closes that round and shows the notes in both colours.

1. Export everything

The .xlsx is the dataset: causes, what-we-would-do (existing or new, each with why it would work), a crosstab by gap × team × role, the gaps, and who was there. Every row carries team, role, setting and the anonymous phone id. The .txt is the same notes grouped under each gap, for reading or for an AI. The practice round is in none of them.

2. A first-draft summary per gap

Analysis, not something for the room. The prompt carries the instructions and every note; it asks the AI for a first-pass synthesis per gap — causes, then what to do — kept separate by team, citing note labels so every claim is traceable. Copy, paste into Claude or ChatGPT. Treat the result as a draft to code from, not as findings.
